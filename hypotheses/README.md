# Hypothesis ledger

This ledger records live ideas to investigate, not conclusions. The initial reading-list “Framework” has been written as candidate propositions in `initial-framework.csv`. They belong to the researcher and should not be attributed to Islam, Muslims, or any cited author without specific evidence.

For each new hypothesis, write a clear, scoped, falsifiable or otherwise assessable proposition; explain its mechanism; say what observations or arguments would count against it; identify serious alternatives; and specify a feasible method and source base. State when a question is metaphysical or normative and cannot be decided by empirical observation alone.

Statuses: `proposed`, `under_review`, `supported_in_scope`, `challenged`, `revised`, `retired`. A status reflects the present evidence in the stated scope, not timeless truth. Preserve prior versions and explain changes.

Do not collapse these into one variable: a source's content, a historian's account of what happened, an interpreter's theological reading, and the researcher's evaluation are different claims.
