## Research question

How do the researcher's standpoint, translations, modern categories, and source selection shape conclusions about Islam?

## Questions

- Which starting beliefs are historical, empirical, interpretive, metaphysical, or normative?
- What evidence or argument could change each position?
- Which categories are used by a source itself, and which are imposed by a modern interpreter?
- How do Arabic terms and translation choices affect the analysis?
- Where are insider, outsider, and disciplinary standpoints visible in the literature?

## Method

Maintain a dated reflexive note for consequential changes. Compare translations and specialist interpretations. Record limits and uncertainty without treating every interpretation as equally supported.
