## Goal

Test the researcher's ten provisional positions in `data/initial-framework.csv` against relevant Islamic texts, traditions, and scholarship.

## Rules

- Treat each position as the researcher's starting view, not as a neutral description of Islam.
- Separate metaphysical commitments from historical, empirical, interpretive, and normative propositions.
- Identify evidence that would change or refine each position before collecting evidence.
- Include strong Muslim interpretations and serious counterarguments rather than only sympathetic comparisons.
- Do not force an empirical test onto a metaphysical or normative claim.
- Preserve dated revisions and unresolved tensions in the graph.

## Initial positions

Ontology; metaphysics; epistemology; revelation; prophets; divine intention; ethics; responsibility; justice; religious belonging.

## Completion criteria

- Each position has a scoped statement and epistemic kind.
- Relevant sources and counterarguments are linked with precise locators.
- The graph distinguishes the researcher's inference from the claims of sources and interpreters.
