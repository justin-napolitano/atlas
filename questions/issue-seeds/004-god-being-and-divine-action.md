## Research question

How do selected Islamic theological, philosophical, and mystical writers describe divine unity, existence, creation, causality, and divine action?

## Scope

Begin by selecting a small, historically identified set of authors from at least two intellectual traditions. Record works, period, genre, translator, and scholarly context. Compare terminology in context; do not assume that “Being,” “existence,” “God,” and “Nature” are interchangeable.

## Questions

- What do these writers mean by divine unity and by God's relation to creation?
- Which claims concern divine attributes, causality, or human language about God?
- How does the researcher's “God is Being” position compare with each author's own terms and arguments?
- What counter-readings challenge a simple equivalence between Islamic metaphysics and Spinozism?

## Method

Use primary texts with named translations and specialist scholarship. Map arguments with premises, conclusions, assumptions, and disagreement; label metaphysical claims as such.
