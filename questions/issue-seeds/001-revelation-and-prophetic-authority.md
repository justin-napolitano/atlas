## Research question

How do selected Qur'anic passages present revelation and Muhammad's authority, and how have Muslim interpreters explained the relationship between the Qur'an, the Messenger, and later religious authority?

## Initial scope

- Begin with Qur'an 4:58–60 and 16:43–44; expand only when the close reading identifies a reason.
- Compare the passage wording and immediate literary context with a named translation and scholarly exegesis.
- Track Sunni and Shi'i interpretive sources separately where the texts support the comparison. Add other traditions when relevant.
- Distinguish the Qur'anic text, later hadith and legal traditions, theological interpretation, and this project's analysis.

## Questions to resolve

- What does the wording attribute to God, the Messenger, and "those in authority" in 4:59?
- What does 4:59 say about resolving disagreement, and how do interpreters understand the referents?
- Who are the "people of remembrance" in 16:43–44 within the passage's context, and how have interpreters identified them?
- Which claims concern the text's wording, and which depend on later reports or interpretive traditions?
- What would challenge the researcher's starting assumption that prophetic status does not confer a distinct kind of epistemic authority?

## Initial scholarly leads

- The Qur'an itself: record the edition/translation and exact verses consulted.
- *The Cambridge Companion to the Qur'an*, especially its discussions of revelation, interpretation, and the Qur'an's historical context: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/the-cambridge-companion-to-the-quran/C396A0E0B9B95A6BE0B560EE0949E4C4
- *The Cambridge Companion to Classical Islamic Theology*, for contextual scholarship on revelation, reason, scriptural interpretation, and theology: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/cambridge-companion-to-classical-islamic-theology/37EC62C7C84B2A8A62C2F6B9AAEDF22D
- *The Oxford Handbook of Divine Revelation*, chapter "Divine Revelation in Islam": https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/34237/chapter-abstract/290277798

These are starting leads; verify full chapter metadata, relevant sections, editions, and competing scholarship before making claims from them. OpenAlex can help discover related works, but citation counts are not a quality ranking.

## Deliverables

- Source notes with exact passage and page locators.
- Separate source-attributed claims from interpretations and researcher inferences.
- Record at least one serious counter-reading for each interpretive claim.
- Add the sourced nodes and provenance to `graph/research.ttl`; preserve unresolved disagreement.
