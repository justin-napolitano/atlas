## Goal

Turn the 36 workbook entries into a verified, usable bibliography without silently treating the workbook's framing as established scholarship.

## Work

- Check author, exact title, edition, publication year, translator/editor, publisher, and stable identifier against publisher/catalog records.
- Mark each item primary text, specialist scholarship, overview, comparison, literature, or other.
- Add an OpenAlex Work ID and a dated citation-count snapshot where found and useful.
- Note language, period, tradition, and question relevance.
- Flag records with mismatched links or uncertain bibliographic details for correction.
- Keep discovery metadata separate from quality judgments; assess relevance, source role, edition, method, specialist context, and perspective gaps separately.

## Completion criteria

- Corrections and unresolved records are documented.
- CSV imports remain traceable to the preserved workbook.
- No source is described as read or verified before checking it.
