## Research question

How have Muslim communities and interpreters understood religious difference, belonging, and pluralism across particular historical settings?

## Scope

Define which communities, texts, places, and periods are under study. Avoid generalizing from a single thinker, state, legal school, or modern debate to all Muslims.

## Questions

- What claims about religious difference appear in the selected sources, and who is speaking?
- How do theological, legal, political, and social settings shape the interpretation?
- How do lived practices and institutions compare with abstract accounts of pluralism?
- When does a modern universalist reading align with a source, and when does specialist scholarship challenge it?

## Method

Pair primary sources with historical and specialist studies. Record reception and context as distinct graph relationships; preserve conflicting interpretations.
