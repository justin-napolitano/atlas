## Research question

How do selected Islamic sources and schools reason about moral knowledge, obligation, justice, human agency, and responsibility?

## Scope

Choose a bounded ethical problem and named authors/traditions before comparing. Track period, genre, legal/theological context, and translation.

## Questions

- What roles do revelation, reason, intention, welfare, virtue, law, and consequences play in the selected accounts?
- What does human responsibility mean in each account, and what limits or conditions are specified?
- How do positions differ across the selected authors or schools?
- Which parts of the researcher's constructivist framework are challenged, supported, or left unresolved?

## Method

Map arguments and their textual evidence. Keep descriptive claims about what authors say separate from normative evaluations. Include objections and counterevidence.
